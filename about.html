About StoryNest

A small library for big feelings, built as a Wattpad-style storybook website.

Our idea

StoryNest collects original short stories that can be finished in a single sitting. Every book has exactly one chapter, so there is never a cliffhanger you cannot resolve before bedtime. The collection spans thirty different genres, from Fantasy and Mystery to Folklore and Fable.

What you can do here

  • Browse 30 covers and filter by genre or search by title and author.
  • Open the book details popup for a quick look before you read.
  • Read each story on its own page, with a reading progress bar.
  • Show or hide the synopsis, like your favorites, and switch between dark and light mode.

Every story includes

A book title, author, genre, synopsis, chapter title, the full story content, and at least one supporting illustration. All covers and illustrations were created for this project.

Built with

Semantic HTML5, one shared CSS file using Flexbox and Grid, and one external JavaScript file. The layout is responsive for desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.
