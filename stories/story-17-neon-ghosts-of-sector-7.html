Cyberpunk

Neon Ghosts of Sector 7

by Jinx Okafor

  • Genre: Cyberpunk
  • Chapter: Packet Loss
  • Reading time: about 1 min

Synopsis

Courier and street hacker Kiro is hired to deliver a memory chip across Sector 7, and the memory on it is her own.

Chapter 1 of 1

Packet Loss

Rain hit the neon like static. Kiro weaved her bike through the crowded lanes of Sector 7, the chip taped inside her jacket, her visor flashing delivery directions. It was just another run: pick up at the noodle stall, drop at the rooftop garden, take the money, disappear.

At the third checkpoint, the chip started to hum. She should not have looked. The fixer had said never to look. But the humming turned into a whisper, and the whisper into a voice, and the voice was hers, saying, "If you're hearing this, I couldn't finish it. Don't trust Mr. Vance."

Kiro skidded to a stop under a flickering sign. She had no memory of recording that. She plugged the chip into her wrist-port and saw her own face, older, bruised, standing in front of a vault. "They erased me once," her recorded self said. "I made a backup. Open the gray door on Level Nine."

The rooftop garden could wait. Kiro turned her bike toward the sky-lifts, the rain streaming off her visor like data. Somewhere above, a gray door waited, and behind it, the girl she used to be.

— The End —
