Compass of the Coral Coast

by Kai Navarro

  • Genre: Adventure
  • Chapter: The Needle That Pointed Home
  • Reading time: about 1 min

Synopsis

Siblings Nico and Bea inherit a broken compass that points not north, but toward whatever you miss the most. Their search leads to a drowned lighthouse.

Chapter 1 of 1

The Needle That Pointed Home

Grandpa Lito's compass had never pointed north, and Nico had always assumed it was broken. Then, on the morning after the funeral, he noticed the needle trembling toward the sea, toward the shoreline where Grandpa used to mend his nets.

"It points to whatever you miss most," his sister Bea said, reading the tiny engraving on the back. They borrowed a rowboat and followed the needle past the reef, past the gulls, past the point where the water turned a deep and secret blue. Underneath them, shapes began to rise: a crooked tower with barnacles on its windows, a lighthouse that had sunk long before they were born.

They tied up at its roof and climbed down through a hatch. Inside, air bubbles shimmered like silver coins. On a shelf, wrapped in oilcloth, sat a wooden box. Inside were a dozen letters addressed to "My Two Navigators," and a map of every fishing spot Grandpa had ever loved, each marked with a tiny heart.

Bea read the first letter aloud, voice cracking. Nico held the compass steady. For the first time, the needle stopped trembling and pointed straight at both of them.
