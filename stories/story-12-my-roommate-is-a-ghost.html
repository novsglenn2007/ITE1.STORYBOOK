My Roommate Is a Ghost

by Tessa Moon

  • Genre: Paranormal
  • Chapter: The Rent Is Suspiciously Cheap
  • Reading time: about 1 min

Synopsis

College sophomore Jade finally finds a cheap apartment, only to discover her roommate has been dead since 1974 and has strong opinions about her dishes.

Chapter 1 of 1

The Rent Is Suspiciously Cheap

The listing said "charming vintage unit, furnished, utilities included," and Jade, who was broke, did not ask why the rent was only forty dollars. On the first night, every cabinet door in the kitchen swung open at the same time. On the second, the faucet turned itself on and a ghostly voice sighed, "Rinse the plates, dear. Rinse."

On the third night, Jade turned around and there she was: a woman in a pale green dress and cat-eye glasses, floating a few inches above the carpet, arms folded. "I'm Odette," said the ghost. "I've lived here since 1974, and you are the messiest person I have ever haunted."

Jade, to her own surprise, did not scream. She said, "Do you know anything about organic chemistry?" Odette brightened. "I was a pharmacist. Sit down."

By midterms, they had a system. Odette taught her the periodic table in rhymes; Jade played her old records and cleaned the stove. When Jade passed her exam, the apartment lights flickered in celebration. "Don't get sentimental," Odette sniffed, wiping a spectral tear. "You still owe me forty dollars."

— The End —
