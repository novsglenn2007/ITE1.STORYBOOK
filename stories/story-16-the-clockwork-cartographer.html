The Clockwork Cartographer

by Ada Fenwick

  • Genre: Steampunk
  • Chapter: Gears and Grid Lines
  • Reading time: about 1 min

Synopsis

Inventor's apprentice Tilly builds a brass mapmaking automaton, only to find it has been drawing a city that does not exist, yet.

Chapter 1 of 1

Gears and Grid Lines

In the soot-streaked workshop above Pennywhistle Lane, Tilly Brass wound the last spring of her masterpiece and stepped back. The Cartographer stood six feet tall, all brass and glass, with a mechanical hand that held a fine ink pen. Its job was simple: to map the streets of Cogsworth City, which changed so often that no human could keep up.

It whirred, clicked, and began to draw. Hours passed. Tilly fell asleep on a pile of blueprints and woke to find the machine still at work, surrounded by hundreds of beautiful maps. But they were not maps of Cogsworth. The streets curved differently. The towers floated on delicate chains. There was a river that Tilly had never seen, lined with silver bridges and little lamps.

"Where is this?" she asked. The Cartographer paused. From a slot in its chest, a thin paper strip unrolled: "Not where. When." The final page was dated fifty years ahead, with one building circled: a small brass workshop, with her name above the door.

Tilly laughed, a little breathless. Then she rolled up her sleeves, because maps are only invitations, and she was never one to refuse a good one.
