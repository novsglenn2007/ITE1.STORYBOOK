Magical Realism

The Woman Who Sold Weather

by Camila Torres

  • Genre: Magical Realism
  • Chapter: A Pinch of Rain
  • Reading time: about 1 min

Synopsis

In a dusty plaza, Doña Alma sells jars of weather, a spoonful of drizzle, a pocket of sunshine, but never to anyone who wants to hide from their own feelings.

Chapter 1 of 1

A Pinch of Rain

On Tuesdays, Doña Alma set up her wooden cart in the plaza, and the whole town came to see what she was selling. Her jars glowed with small, trapped skies: a morning fog for people who liked to think, a handful of snow for children who had never seen any, and tiny thunderclouds for those who needed a good cry.

Nobody asked how she did it. In that town, a woman could sell weather the way another sold oranges, and it seemed perfectly reasonable.

One afternoon, a stiff-backed businessman marched up and demanded, "Give me a jar of sunshine. All of it. I never want to feel gloomy again." Doña Alma studied him with kind, amused eyes. "Every person needs a little rain, señor," she said. "Even the roses complain without it." He insisted, so she handed him the sunniest jar she had.

Three days later, he came back, pale and squinting. "It's too bright," he whispered. "I can't sleep. I can't even feel sad about my mother." Doña Alma nodded, set a tiny drizzle in his palm, and said, "Welcome back." He cried softly, and outside, the rain began, as if the sky had been waiting.
