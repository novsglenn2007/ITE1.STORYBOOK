Slice of Life

The Corner Bakery at 5 A.M.

by Hana Sato

  • Genre: Slice of Life
  • Chapter: Flour and First Light
  • Reading time: about 1 min

Synopsis

Before the city wakes, a baker, a delivery boy, and a stray cat share the quietest hour of the day over warm bread.

Chapter 1 of 1

Flour and First Light

At five o'clock, the street outside Mrs. Aquino's bakery was still blue and sleepy. Inside, the ovens glowed like small suns, and the air smelled like butter, sugar, and the promise of a good day. Mrs. Aquino hummed as she shaped each roll, her hands quick from forty years of practice.

The back door creaked at 5:10. It was Dodong, the delivery boy, yawning, his bicycle basket empty and ready. "Same as always?" he asked. "Same as always," she answered, and handed him a paper bag of pandesal still warm enough to fog his glasses. He ate one roll on the spot, burning his tongue and not caring.

At 5:20, a gray cat padded in through the gap by the flour sacks. Nobody remembered inviting him, but he had a saucer by the stove now, filled every morning with a little milk. He sat beside it with great dignity and waited.

By six, the sky turned pink. The first customers lined up, rubbing their eyes. Mrs. Aquino flipped the sign to OPEN, and the little bell above the door rang. It was an ordinary morning, and she loved every minute of it, down to the last crumb.

— The End —
