Seeds in the Ash

by Ember Walsh

  • Genre: Post-Apocalyptic
  • Chapter: One Packet, One Chance
  • Reading time: about 1 min

Synopsis

Thirteen years after the Collapse, a scavenger named Ysa finds a single sealed packet of seeds and has to decide whether to sell it or plant it.

Chapter 1 of 1

One Packet, One Chance

The world had been gray for as long as Ysa could remember. Ash drifted over the ruined highways like slow snow, and every living thing she met had learned to be small and quiet. She scavenged the old supermarkets for cans, batteries, and anything that still worked.

Today, in a collapsed greenhouse, beneath a rotted shelf, she found a metal tin. Inside lay a paper packet, still sealed, with a faded picture of tomatoes. Seeds. Real seeds. In the market at Haven's Gate, a packet like that could buy a year of food, a safe room, and a guard.

Ysa sat on a broken wall for a long time. Her stomach growled. Her boots had holes. Then she thought of the old woman who had raised her, who used to say that a seed is just a promise folded small.

She dug a hole in the ash behind the greenhouse, poured water from her last bottle, and pressed three seeds into the dirt. Then she waited, hands folded, watching the sky. It was a ridiculous thing to do. But two weeks later, through the gray, a tiny green curl broke the surface, bright as a flame.

— The End —
