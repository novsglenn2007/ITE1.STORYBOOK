Two Umbrellas, One Rainy Street

by Lena Marisol

  • Genre: Romance
  • Chapter: Thursday, Bus Stop 14
  • Reading time: about 1 min

Synopsis

Every rainy Thursday, Ines and Theo share the same bus stop and pretend not to notice each other, until the day only one umbrella survives the storm.

Chapter 1 of 1

Thursday, Bus Stop 14

Ines had a theory that the universe only schedules brave things on rainy days. That was why, every Thursday for eleven weeks, she stood at Bus Stop 14 under a yellow umbrella and carefully did not look at the boy with the green one.

His name was Theo. She knew this because he once told the driver, and she knew he read paperbacks with cracked spines, and she knew he always let the elderly onto the bus first. What she did not know was how to say any of it out loud.

Then the storm came sideways. A gust turned her umbrella inside out and snapped two of its ribs, and Ines stood soaked and speechless as the wind carried it down the street. A green umbrella slid over her head. "I'm Theo," he said. "I'd offer you a better line, but I've been practicing this one for eleven weeks, and it's the only one that survived."

Ines laughed so hard she had to hold his sleeve. They missed the bus. They did not mind. The next Thursday it was sunny, and they both showed up anyway, each holding an umbrella they did not need.
