Space Opera

Crown of a Thousand Suns

by Orion Ashby

  • Genre: Space Opera
  • Chapter: The Girl in Cargo Bay Three
  • Reading time: about 1 min

Synopsis

A teenage deckhand on a smuggler's freighter discovers she is the last heir to the Solar Throne, and the empire's fleets are already hunting her.

Chapter 1 of 1

The Girl in Cargo Bay Three

Zara Venn had spent her whole life scrubbing floors on the freighter Tumbleweed, so when three imperial cruisers dropped out of hyperspace and demanded her by name, she assumed there had been some mistake. "I'm nobody," she told Captain Rook, wiping grease off her chin. "I'm the one who fixes the coffee machine."

Captain Rook, a scarred old smuggler with a heart four sizes bigger than he admitted, pulled a silver circlet out of a locked drawer. "Your mother gave me this the night she died," he said. "I swore to keep you hidden until you were ready. I guess we're out of time."

The circlet glowed when it touched her hair. Star charts blazed across the walls, thousands of systems, each one blinking in recognition. "Every world in the Concord," said Rook quietly, "belongs to the heir of the Solar Throne. That's you, kid."

The cruisers opened fire. The Tumbleweed shuddered. Zara looked at the circlet, then at the controls, then at the crew who had been her family. "Hit the engines," she said, and her voice rang with something older than fear. "We're going to need a bigger plan."
