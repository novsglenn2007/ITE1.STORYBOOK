The Door in the Orchard

by Eliza Thorne

  • Genre: Supernatural
  • Chapter: Between the Apple Trees
  • Reading time: about 1 min

Synopsis

When Isa inherits her grandmother's orchard, she finds a door standing alone between two apple trees, and it opens a little wider each night.

Chapter 1 of 1

Between the Apple Trees

The orchard had been in Isa's family for six generations, and in all that time nobody had mentioned the door. It stood between two ancient apple trees at the far edge of the property: tall, wooden, painted a faded blue, with no walls on either side. From the front it looked ridiculous. From the side, it looked like it was waiting.

On the first night, Isa saw the knob turn slightly. On the second, it opened a finger's width, letting out a thread of warm light and the faint smell of cinnamon. On the third, she took a flashlight, a rolling pin, and a great deal of courage, and she pulled it all the way open.

Beyond it was her grandmother's kitchen, exactly as it had been when Isa was small, with the kettle on, the radio humming, and flour dusting the table. Her grandmother stood at the stove, younger than Isa had ever known her, and without turning she said, "You're late for supper, apple blossom."

Isa stepped through, tears on her cheeks, and the door swung quietly shut behind her. In the morning, the orchard was in bloom, and from somewhere inside it came the soft sound of someone laughing.
