Forty Minutes to Midnight

by Dante Holloway

  • Genre: Thriller
  • Chapter: Platform Nine
  • Reading time: about 1 min

Synopsis

A train engineer receives an anonymous message: a bomb is on board, and the only person who can stop it is the one passenger he has never met.

Chapter 1 of 1

Platform Nine

Engineer Rafi Santos had driven the 11:20 night express for fourteen years, and in all that time his phone had never buzzed during a run. At 11:21 it buzzed twice. The message was short: "There is a device on your train. Forty minutes. Do not stop. Do not call. Find Seat 31C."

Rafi's hands stayed steady on the throttle, which surprised him. He radioed a routine delay to dispatch, asked the conductor to cover the front car, and walked the aisle as casually as a man checking tickets. Seat 31C held a sleeping teenager in headphones, a violin case across her knees.

"Miss," Rafi whispered, "I need you to open that case." She did, with a puzzled frown, and inside lay a violin, a spare string, and a note taped to the lid in the same blunt handwriting as the message: "Play the song your grandmother taught you."

The girl's face went white. "How do they know about that song?" Then she lifted the violin and began to play. Somewhere under the floor, a faint electronic chirp slowed, stuttered, and stopped. Rafi leaned on the seat back, finally breathing, and wondered who had written a bomb that listened for lullabies.
