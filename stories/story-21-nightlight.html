Nightlight

by Marcus Reed

  • Chapter: Lights Out, Lights On
  • Reading time: about 1 min

Synopsis

Ten-year-old Dev is scared of the dark, so naturally he gains the power to glow, and the city's blackout needs him badly.

Chapter 1 of 1

Lights Out, Lights On

The blackout began at 8:07 p.m. and swallowed the entire city of Brightwater, which was ironic, because Dev Patel was ten years old and had been secretly sleeping with a nightlight for as long as he could remember. That night, with the power out and his lamp dead, he curled up in bed and whispered, "Please, I just want some light."

His hands started to glow. Soft at first, like fireflies, then brighter, until the whole room was lit up like a lantern. Dev stared. "Okay," he whispered. "That's new."

Outside, sirens wailed, and people stumbled in the dark. Dev ran into the street, his glowing hands lifted high. A lost toddler stopped crying. A bus driver found the road. An old man on crutches reached the curb safely. Everywhere Dev walked, the dark backed away, and people followed him like moths.

By midnight, he had led half the neighborhood to the shelter at the school gym, where he sat on a stack of mats, exhausted and proud. A reporter asked for his name. "Nightlight," he said, then yawned. "Because I'm not afraid of the dark anymore. I'm the one who brings the light."

— The End —
