The Honest Thief

by Gideon Park

  • Genre: Crime
  • Chapter: Nothing Personal
  • Reading time: about 1 min

Synopsis

Master burglar Lou Pereira only steals from people who deserve it, and tonight's target has a safe full of someone else's secrets.

Chapter 1 of 1

Nothing Personal

Lou Pereira had a code. He never stole from families, never broke anything he didn't have to, and always left a thank-you note in the empty drawer. His targets, he liked to say, were people who could afford the lesson.

Tonight's target was a penthouse on Ocean Avenue, owned by Garrett Voss, a developer who evicted old neighbors to build glass towers. The lock on the study was easy. The safe took four minutes. Inside, instead of jewels, lay a stack of folders, each labeled with a name, each filled with photographs and bank records.

Lou flipped through them. They were blackmail files. Dozens of them. A city councilwoman, a retired judge, a school principal, all trapped in Voss's quiet little net. Lou frowned, because that was not part of the code.

He photographed every page, slid the folders back, and left his usual note on the desk. Only this time, the note read: "Check your mail tomorrow. So will the police." Then he disappeared into the night, whistling. The next morning, forty-one envelopes arrived at forty-one addresses, each containing a single folder and a short, cheerful line: "Nothing personal. You're free now."
