Detective Noir

Rain Never Lies in Harbor City

by Frank Delacroix

  • Genre: Detective Noir
  • Chapter: The Dame in the Downpour
  • Reading time: about 1 min

Synopsis

Private eye Sam Quill takes one last case from a client who pays in damp cash and a name he has not heard in ten years.

Chapter 1 of 1

The Dame in the Downpour

It was raining in Harbor City, which meant it was a Tuesday. I was sitting in my office with a cold coffee and a warm bottle of regret when she walked in, shaking water from a red umbrella. She had the kind of face that arrived before the trouble.

"Mr. Quill?" she said. "I need you to find my husband." I asked for a name. She gave one, and the coffee went colder. It belonged to a man who had been dead for ten years. I knew because I had watched them lower him into the ground.

"He sends me notes," she said. "Always signed with a little key." She slid an envelope across the desk. Inside was a key, brass, worn smooth, stamped with a number: 409. The same number as the pier warehouse where it all went wrong a decade ago.

I put on my coat. "Stay here," I said. She didn't. Of course she didn't. We walked into the rain together, two strangers carrying the same ghost, and somewhere between the neon and the water I realized the key did not open a door. It opened me.

— The End —
