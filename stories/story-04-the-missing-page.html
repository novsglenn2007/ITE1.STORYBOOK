The Missing Page

by Oren Blackwood

  • Genre: Mystery
  • Chapter: Page One Hundred and Fourteen
  • Reading time: about 1 min

Synopsis

When page 114 vanishes from the town library's only copy of a centuries-old diary, young librarian Pia must find out who needed it gone.

Chapter 1 of 1

Page One Hundred and Fourteen

The Marlowe Diary had survived two fires, a flood, and a mayor who wanted it burned. It could not survive Tuesday. Pia opened the glass case that morning and found the book intact, every page in place except one: page 114, cut out so neatly that she had to run her thumb down the spine to feel the gap.

She checked the visitor log. Three names: a schoolteacher, a retired fisherman, and a man who had signed only with a tidy squiggle. She checked the camera. It had recorded nothing but the clock, which stood at 3:07 for the entire day.

Pia thought like a librarian. Whoever took the page did not want the diary; they wanted what it said. She searched the catalog for anything quoting page 114 and found a single reference in a century-old map of the harbor. Rolled up inside that map's tube was a small brass key and a note: "Under the third bell."

Pia climbed the church tower with her heart hammering. Beneath the third bell, taped to the wood, was page 114, and beside it, in modern ink, one line: "Thank you for looking. Now you know why the harbor was never sold." She read the page twice, and then, very carefully, she smiled.
