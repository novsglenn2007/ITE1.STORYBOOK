The Karaoke Machine That Wouldn't Quit

by Joy Mendoza

  • Genre: Comedy
  • Chapter: Hit It, Tita!
  • Reading time: about 1 min

Synopsis

At Tita Nena's birthday party, the old karaoke machine refuses to stop, picks every song itself, and seems determined to expose everyone's secrets.

Chapter 1 of 1

Hit It, Tita!

The family reunion was going perfectly until Tita Nena plugged in the karaoke machine. It was older than the youngest cousin, wrapped in duct tape, and had a sticker on the side that said DO NOT TOUCH (LOLO MEANT IT). Nobody listened.

The first song began on its own: a dramatic ballad about a lost love named Rodrigo. Tito Boy turned the color of a ripe tomato. "That's an old song," he said loudly. "Very old. Nothing to do with anyone." The machine skipped to a second song, "Why Did You Eat My Lechon?" and Cousin Paolo suddenly found the ceiling fascinating.

By the fifth track, the whole family had joined in, because nothing unites relatives like public embarrassment. Lola Pacing sang so loudly her dentures made a break for freedom. Tita Nena grabbed a ladle for a microphone and delivered a high note that cracked two glasses and, apparently, the machine's pride.

With a final sad beep, it fell silent. Everybody applauded. "Best party ever," whispered Paolo, wiping tears from laughing. On the little screen, one last line glowed: "Same time next year?" Tita Nena unplugged it, kissed its dusty top, and said, "Obviously."

— The End —
