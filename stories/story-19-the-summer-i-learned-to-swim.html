Coming-of-Age

The Summer I Learned to Swim

by Noah Bellamy

  • Genre: Coming-of-Age
  • Chapter: The Deep End of July
  • Reading time: about 1 min

Synopsis

Fifteen-year-old Marco is afraid of deep water, and the lake his cousins love is full of it. One summer, he decides to stop watching from the dock.

Chapter 1 of 1

The Deep End of July

Everyone in Marco's family swam as if they had been born with fins. His cousins cannonballed off the dock, his uncle crossed the lake before breakfast, and even his little sister paddled like a puppy. Marco sat at the end of the pier with a towel on his shoulders, pretending that sunburn was a hobby.

"You can't read your way across a lake," teased his cousin Dani, flicking water at him. Marco laughed it off, but at night he lay awake listening to the crickets and thinking about the dark, still water. Fear, he decided, was just a very loud thought.

On the last Saturday of July, he walked down to the shore alone. He stepped in up to his ankles, then his knees, then his chest. His heart pounded. A small wave nudged him forward, and for one scary second his feet left the bottom. He kicked. He sputtered. He floated.

When he finally turned back, the whole family was standing on the dock, silent, watching. Then Dani shouted, "Marco's swimming!" and they all cheered like a stadium. Marco grinned, water in his ears, sun on his face, no longer on the outside of anything.

— The End —
