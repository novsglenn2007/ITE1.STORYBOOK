Dust at Redrock Junction

by Cole Harlan

  • Genre: Western
  • Chapter: High Noon, Low Water
  • Reading time: about 1 min

Synopsis

A tired sheriff, a missing water wagon, and one hot afternoon decide whether Redrock Junction survives the summer.

Chapter 1 of 1

High Noon, Low Water

The sun hung over Redrock Junction like a hammer, and Sheriff June Calloway had not seen water in three days. The town's wagon was overdue, the well was dry, and the only soul who knew why was a drifter named Eli, asleep in her jail cell with his hat over his face.

"I didn't steal it," Eli said when she woke him, boots up on the cot. "I followed it. Somebody's been moving that wagon at night, and they leave tracks like a man with one shoe."

June had a hunch about one-shoe tracks. Hunches were all she had, so she saddled her mare and followed Eli's map across the baked flats to a hidden canyon. There, behind a screen of dry brush, sat the wagon. And beside it stood Mayor Pruitt, counting barrels, wearing one boot and one slipper.

"Evenin', Mayor," said June, leveling her rifle. Pruitt sighed. "I was going to sell it back at triple the price." "That's low," June said, "even for you."

By sundown, the wagon rolled home with the mayor tied to the back. The whole town came out to cheer as the barrels were opened. Eli tipped his hat. June did not smile, but she did hand him the first cup.
